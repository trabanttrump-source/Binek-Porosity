Why Binek?

Porosity Analysis

Percentage, pore size distribution (5 categories), histograms, and colour‑coded fusion images.

Length & Area

Three independent measurement series with statistics (mean, std dev). ROI‑based area calculation.

Hardness Testing

Vickers (HV) and Knoop (HK) with custom loads. Diagonal measurement and automatic calculation.

Regions of Interest

Up to 5 ROIs per image. Rectangle, Ellipse, Polygon & Freeform. Independent binarization per ROI.

Live Camera

DirectShow camera preview and capture. Brightness, contrast & gamma adjustment in real time.

Reports & Export

Word (DOCX) and Excel (XLSX) reports with annotated images. Export PNG, JPG, TIFF and project files (.binek).
